# Open Questions & Deviations

Agents append entries here when a spec is ambiguous, conflicts with an ADR, or does not match an external API's real behavior (see `README.md` → Protocol, step 4). The project owner resolves them. A decision that matters long term becomes a new ADR.

Format:

```markdown
## <spec #> — <topic>
- **Context:** what was unclear or wrong
- **Choice made:** what was implemented
- **Alternatives:** other options considered
- **Status:** open | resolved (<date>, <ADR or spec change>)
```
